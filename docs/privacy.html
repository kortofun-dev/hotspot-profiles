Hotspot Profiles privacy policy

Developer: KORTOFUN

Hotspot Profiles helps you sign in to a compatible Infinity hotspot, check your allowance, and end your session. It does not create a separate Hotspot Profiles account.

Saved profiles and settings

The app stores your saved hotspot profiles and passwords on your device using Android-backed secure storage. It also keeps settings, usage history, and a limited diagnostic history on the device. Android backup for the app is disabled.

Infinity hotspot

When the app signs in, checks a session, or logs out, it communicates with the Infinity portal or gateway over HTTPS. The portal receives the account and session information needed to provide those functions and may store cookies in the embedded browser. The hotspot provider handles the account, session, and network traffic under its own terms and privacy practices.

Android permissions

If you grant location permission to show the current Wi-Fi network name, the app uses it to read the network name on your device. It does not use that permission to read your geographic coordinates. Exact alarms and a foreground service support the optional logout timer. Notifications can show that automatic logout protection is active.

Feedback and diagnostics

Diagnostics stay on your device until you choose to copy a report or continue to GitHub. Continuing to GitHub opens a link containing your description and, when selected, the report you can preview in the app. The report can include device model, Android and WebView versions, recent diagnostic events, and logout outcomes. The generated report excludes passwords, hotspot account names, network names, IP addresses, and session IDs. Avoid putting personal information in the description. GitHub handles the link and any issue you submit. A public issue can show the contents of your report and your GitHub account.

Retention and deletion

You can remove a saved profile in the app. To remove all local app data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. To manage data held by the hotspot provider or GitHub, use their respective account and support tools.

Contact

Contact KORTOFUN through the GitHub project or the feedback option in Settings. For privacy questions, leave account details and other personal information out of public issues.
